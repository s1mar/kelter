## Datasets

One row per dataset, as written by the converters.

| Dataset | Hugging Face id | License | Split role |
|---|---|---|---|
| BANKING77 | legacy-datasets/banking77 (parquet copy of PolyAI/banking77) | cc-by-4.0 | train |
| CLINC150 | clinc/clinc_oos, config plus | cc-by-3.0 | new-source holdout (calib, test); oos items are null |
| MASSIVE en-US | AmazonScience/massive, refs/convert/parquet | cc-by-4.0 | train |
| PAWS labeled_final | google-research-datasets/paws | card says "other" | train |
| Civil Comments | google/civil_comments | cc0-1.0 | train (a third re-asked as 4-level score) |
| BoolQ | google/boolq | cc-by-sa-3.0 | train |
| MultiNLI | nyu-mll/multi_nli | card lists cc-by-3.0, cc-by-sa-3.0, mit, other (OANC license for most text) | train |
| ANLI | facebook/anli | cc-by-nc-4.0 | evaluation only, never trained on |
| e-SNLI | github.com/OanaMariaCamburu/e-SNLI CSVs | repo LICENSE has MIT terms; SNLI sentences are CC BY-SA 4.0 | train; test split is the witness evaluation |
| SQuAD 2.0 | rajpurkar/squad_v2 | CC BY-SA 4.0 | train; test half is the witness evaluation |
| ARC-Easy | allenai/ai2_arc | cc-by-sa-4.0 | train |
| ARC-Challenge | allenai/ai2_arc | cc-by-sa-4.0 | new-source holdout (calib, test) |
| MMLU | cais/mmlu, config all | MIT | train (2000 seeded test items), dev split as dev |
| Synthetic policy and score tasks | generated by kelter/data/synthetic.py and synthetic_score.py | this repository | train; the "sharing" policy family is a new-source holdout |
| JevBench public tiers | fstandhartinger/jevbench datasets/public | MIT | test only, never trained or calibrated on |

Share-alike (CC BY-SA) and the PAWS "other" license apply to the data. Whether they reach the trained weights is a legal question to settle before redistributing them more widely.
